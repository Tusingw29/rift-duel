# SPACING-COMMITMENT (2026-10-28)

Owner Codex; carried out by Claude at Adam's request. Branch `codex/spacing-commitment`.

**Status: Review, awaiting Adam's normal-speed verdict.** Everything is behind URL switches. With no switch the game is identical to main: the same rules object and the same line-100 switch point, and the test suite checks a state fingerprint taken on main (all 27 sandbox recipes for three pairings, plus an attack/capture sweep from 1.5 to 5.0 units for every fighter). No damage values were changed.

| Switch | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| *(none)* | Today's game |
| `&spacing=band` | In the band just outside reach (`reach ≤ d < reach + 1.0`), a neutral attack commits to a melee strike that can whiff. Beyond the band it fires a shot, as today |
| `&spacing=riskyshot` | The automatic switch to a shot stays, but a neutral shot fired inside that band gets +4 startup and +12 recovery |
| `&guard=split` | A fresh guard tap no longer breaks a capture. A capture is broken by a fresh **capture** tap instead (not while guarding, same 12-frame window), and that tap loses to a strike |

The switches combine (`&spacing=band&guard=split`). They work in the game (`index.html`) and the sandbox. A match keeps the mode it was created with, so replays are exact.

All numbers live in one object, `SPACING_COMMITMENT` in `dist/spacing-commitment.js`. **They are playtest guesses picked from the frame tables, not tuned values:**
- **Band width 1.0:** about what an average fighter (5.3 u/s) walks during a hit-1 startup plus active (13 frames is 1.15 units), rounded down.
- **Riskyshot +4 / +12:** the startup lands near a hit-2 startup and the recovery near a hit-3 recovery.
- **Capture-tech window 12 frames:** the same width the guard break has today, so only the read changes, not the timing difficulty.

**Training floor marker:** with a spacing switch on, training mode draws a solid ring at the player's reach and a dashed ring at the band edge. Each is a thin light line on a dark backing, so it reads on pale stone and dark floors alike (`training-band-marker.jpg`). It never shows without a switch, outside training, or in lessons. The sandbox shows it too when a switch is on.

## Part 2: guard overlap, measured

Measured in the real simulation by `scripts/review/frame-advantage.mjs`, section 1 of [docs/FRAME_ADVANTAGE.md](../../../docs/FRAME_ADVANTAGE.md):

- **Perfect guard** (`guardTime < .17`): guard frames 1–10.
- **Capture break** (`guardTime < .2`): guard frames 1–12. The 12th accumulated step is 0.19999…, just under the threshold.
- **One tap beats both** a hit 1 and a capture started on the same frame:
  - for Aegis, Rei, Morrow, Sol and Vesper, all 10 perfect-guard timings also break the capture;
  - for Tala, 9 of 10.

  The attacker's choice between striking and capturing therefore barely matters against a fresh tap.
- **Held guard** (older than 12 frames): blocks strikes and loses to captures. That part already works as a read.

**Why `guard=split` works this way.** A tighter capture-break window alone doesn't remove the overlap:
- The capture lands only 2–4 frames after a hit 1 started on the same frame. The window would have to shrink to 1–2 frames before the overlap goes away for the slower strikes, which turns it into a one-frame trick.
- Making the perfect guard strikes-only and moving the capture break to the **capture button** removes it completely. This is the strike/throw/guard triangle of 3D fighters:
  - a guard tap beats a strike;
  - a capture tap beats a capture;
  - a strike beats the capture tap, because that tap starts the defender's own 13-frame capture;
  - a capture beats a held guard.
- **Measured under split:**
  - no guard tap breaks a capture;
  - capture taps break it on the same 12 timings the guard tap used to;
  - a capture tap is hit by a strike at every timing from 0 to 20;
  - holding guard while tapping capture does not tech, so the defender has to commit.
- **Kept under split:**
  - the perfect guard's stun and charge, and projectile reflection;
  - the third-hit reversal (tested);
  - the capture break itself, now on a different read.
- **The tech is a break, not a free counter:** the defender's own capture is cancelled.

## Parts 3 and 4: the tables

Full tables: [docs/FRAME_ADVANTAGE.md](../../../docs/FRAME_ADVANTAGE.md). They are regenerated by `node scripts/review/frame-advantage.mjs` (about 30 s), with real inputs: attacks fire on release, and the human reaction is 15 frames.

**Blocked chains: the defender is far ahead after every block.**
- **Frames:** the defender is free 8 frames after any blocked hit (0.12 s block stun). The attacker is free 19–46 frames after. So every blocked hit and finish is −11 to −38, and blocks have no pushback.
- **Stopping always loses** after every blocked hit and finish: every defender's hit 1 lands before the attacker can guard. The one exception is Tala's hit 1 (−11), which only Tala punishes.
- **Continuing** into the next chain hit:
  - trades against the 10-startup defenders;
  - is interrupted by Tala (9 startup);
  - beats Morrow (11).
- **Morrow's chain** is interrupted by everyone, because his hit 2 has 16 startup.
- **No case where continuing or stopping always wins** for the attacker. The capture finish beats a held guard outright, which is by design.
- **Flagged problem:** a held guard plus an immediate hit 1 is the dominant answer to every string.

**Whiff punish (band mode, attacker at reach + 0.05):**
- **On reaction:** 0 of 36 pairings are punishable.
- **Only on a guess:** 31 pairings. The defender has to start moving 5–12 frames before a human could react.
- **Not punishable at all:** 5 pairings, all long reach whiffing against short reach: Rei vs Aegis, Tala and Sol; Morrow vs Tala and Sol.
- **Tala against Morrow:** the gap is 1.05. She walks 11 frames and lands 36 frames after his whiff begins. Morrow can act at 23, and his fresh guard deflects her. That fits the brief's rough 30 vs 21.

**Sol's shot (7 startup, 11 recovery):**
- **Default:** not punishable by anyone from the band, 4 or 6 units. Only Rei can pre-empt it on a guess at the band edge.
- **Riskyshot:** fixes this for only some pairs. Tala's point-blank shot becomes punishable on reaction by everyone, and Aegis's and Vesper's by Rei and Morrow. Rei's, Morrow's and Sol's stay safe or guess-only.

## Proposals (notes only, not applied)

1. **Whiff recovery.** Add recovery only when a band strike whiffs; hits and blocks stay as they are. The measured worst case per attacker makes the whiff punishable on reaction by everyone:

   | Attacker | Extra whiff recovery |
   | --- | --- |
   | Aegis | +9 |
   | Rei | +15 |
   | Tala | +8 |
   | Morrow | +14 |
   | Sol | +7 |
   | Vesper | +10 |

   The longer reaches need more, because the gap to close is bigger.
2. **Block stun and pushback first.** Today guarding wins outright, so the block itself needs changing before whiffs matter much.
   - **First pass to measure:** block stun of about 15–16 frames for hits 1–2 (from 8), plus about 0.4 units of pushback. That should make stopping after hit 1 roughly safe, and make continuing a real guess. Hit 3 and the finishes keep their minus frames.
   - Run `frame-advantage.mjs` again before choosing values.
3. **Riskyshot.** +12 recovery is enough only for Tala, because her band is the closest.
   - Either scale the extra recovery with band distance (the needed extra per shooter is in the last table: up to +18 more for Morrow),
   - or prefer `band` over `riskyshot`, since a melee whiff reads more clearly than a slow shot.
4. **Sol's shot** is safe from 4+ units, and that seems to be his identity. If his point-blank shot should be punishable, riskyshot needs about +8 more recovery for him.
5. **`guard=split`.** I'd keep it: it removes the free overlap and preserves every existing defensive tool.

## Limits

- **Measurement scope:** the frame tables assume a plain held-guard defender, an attacker who holds guard after stopping, and one hit-1 punish. They don't cover circle steps, pursuit or jump-ins as punishes.
- **Finishes:** forward, back and technique finishes differ per fighter (projectiles and areas). They are measured by first contact only.
- **The AI** doesn't use the capture tech under `guard=split`.
- **Release step:** `npm run prepare:release` (the build-hash import map) was not run on this branch. The two new modules load fine without it.
