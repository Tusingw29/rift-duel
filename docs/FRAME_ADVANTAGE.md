# Frame advantage, guard overlap and whiff punishes

Generated by `node scripts/review/frame-advantage.mjs` from the real simulation (`dist/combat.js` `update`, 60 Hz, scripted inputs). Measurement only: SPACING-COMMITMENT changes no default rule. Frames are update steps, so hitstop counts as real frames. Band width 1, riskyshot +4 startup / +12 recovery: playtest guesses, not tuned values.

Input model: attacks fire on the release frame (press, then release one frame later), as in the game. Human reaction is taken as 15 frames.

## 1. Guard overlap (default rules)

Perfect guard counts while `guardTime < .17` (guard frames 1–10). The capture break counts while `guardTime < .2` (guard frames 1–12: the 12th accumulated step is 0.19999…, under the threshold). Below, a guard tap on frame *g* relative to the attacker's press on frame 0, against a hit 1 strike or a capture started on the same frame.

| Attacker | Perfect guard taps | Capture-break taps | One tap beats both | Share of perfect taps that also break |

| Aegis | 2–11 | 2–13 | 2–11 | 10 / 10 |
| Rei | 2–11 | 2–13 | 2–11 | 10 / 10 |
| Tala | 1–10 | 2–13 | 2–10 | 9 / 10 |

| Vesper | 2–11 | 2–13 | 2–11 | 10 / 10 |

A held guard (older than 12 frames) blocks strikes and loses to a capture. A tap inside the overlap beats both with no read.

### With `&guard=split`

A fresh guard keeps its perfect-guard window against strikes and projectiles but no longer breaks a capture. A capture is broken by a single press of the capture button, while not guarding, within 12 frames before it lands. That press starts the defender's own capture (13 startup), so a strike beats it.

## 2. Blocked chains

The attacker starts at distance 2.0 and taps each hit on its first chainable frame against a held guard (a plain block, not a perfect guard). Finishes come out of hit 2 at its cancel frame. *Attacker acts* / *defender acts*: frames after the blocked contact until each side is free (the attacker's chain cancel is earlier; see the continue column). *Advantage* is defender acts minus attacker acts: negative means the defender is free first. Blocks have no pushback today, so the distance is the chain advance only.

*Stop*: the attacker stops and holds guard (a fresh guard once free); the defender swings as soon as it is free. Listed: defenders whose swing lands. *Continue*: the attacker chains on; the defender swings when free. Each defender gets A (attacker's next hit lands first), D (defender interrupts), T (trade) or – (neither lands).

## 3. Whiff punish (`&spacing=band`)

The attacker presses hit 1 from its reach + 0.05 (inside the band, so it whiffs), then holds guard. The defender guards, then from a chosen frame walks in and taps hit 1 once inside its own reach. *Window* is the latest start frame, counted from the attacker's first whiffed active frame, that still lands: **on reaction** if at least 15, **only on a guess** if the defender has to start earlier than a human can react, **not punishable** if no start lands. The attacker's fresh guard as it recovers is a perfect guard, so a late punish is deflected.
