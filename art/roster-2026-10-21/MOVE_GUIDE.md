## Review notes and required cleanup

All twelve images were visually reviewed as returned by the generator. Alpha and dimensions were checked separately in `asset-checks.json`. All pose sheets have actual alpha, but some contain broad partially transparent glow. They are reference atlases, not validated game sprites. Vesper's sheet is 1347×1168 and not divisible into four equal-width pixel cells; do not automatically slice it as a uniform atlas. The other sheets are 1536×1024, but their drawn bounds still require manual checking. No images are loaded by the live game.

- Aegis: option A clearly communicates armor/beam identity. Reduce small armor facets for PS1-scale readability. The generic burst release needs a later dedicated blade-verdict storyboard.
- Rei: coat tails and spirit help silhouette; some cuts become too similar. Preserve weapon-hand continuity and define spirit delay rather than treating the spirit as constant visual clutter.
- Tala: the second and third combo drawings read too similarly. The hook must bend the striking elbow and show a sweeping arc; stance width and torso rotation need a dedicated combo strip before animation.
- Morrow: first draft cell 14 incorrectly reads as a sword rush. Last Horizon remains a missile salvo; use the braced shoulder-launcher silhouette from cell 10 as the basis for its actual burst storyboard. Do not replace the game's move with this generated mismatch.
- Sol: the second strike should use the staff butt, but the drawing still emphasizes the crystal end. His burst needs a separate overhead orb staging sheet with the real camera, not a generic glow.
- Vesper: the floating halo is missing in several action cells and ring ornaments become overly elaborate. Restore the selected halo and simplify ring detail; preserve consistent scale and trailing hair across cells.

These limitations are recorded so attractive concept art is not mistaken for a complete, technically reusable animation pack. Production clips should be redrawn/rigged from the selected direction and checked in motion.
