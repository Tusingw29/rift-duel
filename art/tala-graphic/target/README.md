# Tala — finished model stylescape

[Full-resolution sheet](tala-final-stylescape.png)

New concept art generated with the built-in image tool, using roster direction 15 as the reference. This is the proposed appearance target for the finished 3D model, not a render of the current mesh or an approved technical turnaround. The exact generation prompt is in [prompt.txt](prompt.txt).

## Target

- Adult athletic build, approximately 7.5 heads tall; long functional legs, defined shoulders, natural waist and connected anatomy.
- Angular almond-shaped amber eyes, restrained brows, tapered jaw and natural neck transition. Preserve the same face across front, profile and gameplay views.
- Aub​urn high ponytail with a complete scalp/nape and a few broad, tapered clumps. Hair motion should follow the head without exposing gaps.
- Fitted open ochre sleeveless vest, charcoal athletic crop top, rust shorts, simple dark belt, ivory hand/forearm and shin wraps, flat black martial shoes.
- Large solid color regions and one clear shadow band. Matte surfaces, restrained face contours, no noisy texture or realistic gloss.
- Wrapped hands must show curled fingers, an outside thumb and an aligned wrist. Flat shoes must permit ankle flexion and planted/pivoting support feet.
- Braced guard crosses the forearms in front of the upper body, protects the jaw, tucks the chin and bends the knees. The sheet shows pose ideas, not frame-accurate animations.

## Interpret the generated sheet carefully

The material spheres are too glossy for the chosen flat-color finish; treat them as palette references only. Small belt/hip details vary between views: use one plain belt, without extra hanging straps or pouches. Resolve seam placement and wrapping consistently on the actual model. Turnaround alignment and hand anatomy still require a human/modeling check; this is an art target rather than topology or rigging evidence.

## Comparison with current preview

Priorities are the face/jaw and eye shape, more expressive ponytail silhouette, fitted continuous shoulder/vest construction, shorter shorts with complete thigh coverage, anatomically shaped wrapped fists, and slimmer flat footwear. Preserve the working combat bone contract and damage-frame timing while improving these forms. Review under the actual flat shader from front, side, back and the gameplay camera before extending the style to other characters.
