## Review guidance

Choose the rendering and proportion direction first. These exploratory sheets vary small costume, weapon, and face details; reconcile them with the approved character concepts before model implementation. Morrow retains the oversized Gravebound sword and Tala remains an unarmed fighter. No runtime models changed in this delivery.
