# Alpha 0.36 production art assets

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then used in the actual game.
Original generated PNGs remain in the generator's output directory. The opaque
arena images were copied into the project and encoded as JPEG for download size;
the face retains its original PNG transparency.

- `dist/assets/storm-panorama.jpg`: wrapped painted scenery for Storm Summit.
- `dist/assets/courtyard-stone.jpg`: repeating diffuse ground map.
- `dist/assets/tala-face.png`: painted face on a curved 3D head patch.

## Panorama prompt

Create an original painted panoramic background texture for an actual 1999 PlayStation anime fighting game, inspired by Evil Zone's detailed scenic arenas. Asset, not a mockup: no characters, no text, no HUD, no foreground fighting platform. Very wide 2:1 landscape panorama. Dramatic distant jade mountain ranges, ancient Japanese mountain shrine buildings and red torii gates on rocky cliffs in the middle distance, lush wooded valleys, layered mist, vivid blue sky with hand-painted cumulus clouds. Horizon in the lower half. Broad angular color shapes, rich painted stone and foliage textures, crisp readable landmarks, late-1990s Japanese game background art, detailed enough to replace flat geometric mountain silhouettes. Not minimalist vector art, not photorealistic, not generic mobile game art. Full bleed landscape image with no border. The left and right edges should have similar sky and mountain heights for wrapping around an arena.

## Face prompt

Production game texture asset, not a portrait scene. A straight-on symmetrical face texture for an original young adult female martial artist in a late-1990s Japanese anime PlayStation fighting game inspired by Evil Zone. Only her oval face from forehead to pointed chin, no hair, no ears, no neck, no shoulders, no clothing. Warm medium tan skin. Confident large almond-shaped dark brown anime eyes, dark upper lashes and thin angled eyebrows, tiny painted nose, subtle neutral lips. Original character, not an existing character. Traditional cel-painted anime shading, clear broad color areas and precise facial features, no photorealism, no modern 3D rendering, no gloss, no outlines outside the face. Front-facing orthographic neutral lighting, not tilted. Face fills the image with an oval silhouette. Transparent background outside the face. Intended to map onto the front of a small 3D game head. No text, no borders, no shadows outside the face.

## Ground prompt

Production game texture asset: a seamless tileable square diffuse texture of weathered pale gray limestone courtyard paving for a 1999 PlayStation Japanese anime fighting game. Orthographic straight-down view, no perspective, no horizon, no objects, no text. Irregular medium-sized fitted stone slabs, fine cracks, worn chipped edges, subtle moss in narrow seams, softly painted mineral variation. Muted warm gray stone with some desaturated sage moss. Hand-painted late-1990s game texture aesthetic, readable at low resolution. Avoid a perfect grid, bold dark grout lines, modern photoreal PBR surfaces, dramatic lighting, cast shadows, and borders. Edges must tile seamlessly.
