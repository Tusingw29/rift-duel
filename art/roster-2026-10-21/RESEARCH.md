# Reference study — October 21, 2026

## What was studied

This pass reviewed Evil Zone character imagery, the existing project footage/move research, and period anime design imagery. It is a bounded art-direction study, not exhaustive mastery or measured animation-frame analysis.

- [Evil Zone character ensemble](https://www.speedrun.com/evil_zone) and [game screenshots](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/197247-evil-zone/images): visibly varied armored hero, fantasy, martial and supernatural silhouettes. For production, separate illustrated art from actual gameplay models. The latter need readable silhouettes, painted surface detail and posed anatomy, not merely low polygon counts.
- [Escaflowne, Sunrise official work page](https://www.sunrise-inc.co.jp/international/work/detail.php?cid=118) verifies the 1996 series; [design-sheet archive](https://characterdesignreferences.com/art-of-animation-6/art-of-the-vision-of-escaflowne) supplies visual references. Useful observations: tapered facial profiles, long limb proportions, strong mantle/coat outlines and concise costume color regions. These are our visual interpretations, not claims about Evil Zone's production influences.
- [Gundam Wing, Sunrise official work page](https://www.sunrise-inc.co.jp/international/work/detail.php?cid=88) verifies the 1995 series; [pilot illustration](https://www.zerochan.net/422062) was used to inspect hairstyle and uniform differentiation. Useful interpretation: clear tailored collars, distinct hair masses and readable restrained clothing rather than many tiny decorative parts.
- [Slayers, licensor page](https://www.enoki-films.co.jp/pro_slayers1.php) and [period ensemble illustration](https://ladyrebek.blogspot.com/2018/10/critica-slayers-reena-y-gaudy.html): broad cape/shoulder shapes, expressive angular eyes, warm/cool costume separation and conspicuous magical gestures. Use these principles for Sol and fantasy variants, without copying Lina or another complete character design.
- [Madhouse work index](https://madhouse.co.jp/english/works/) places the original Trigun in 1998. It is additional period context, not the basis of any measured pose study here; current Trigun adaptations must not be confused with the 1998 visual reference.

## Practical art brief

Original adult fighters, mostly 7–7.5 heads tall as a proposed design target (not a measured original-game proportion). Ink contours that taper at facial details; sparse interior lines; hair built from a few directional clumps. Start with base plus one shadow tone, adding small highlights only where they clarify material. Cloth should fold around joints and flow from actual attachment points. Armor has fitted volumes and visible flexible gaps. Keep the roster's faces and silhouettes distinguishable without color.

The current generated boards are exploratory. Some have more modern surface detail and longer proportions than the intended in-game target. Aegis armor and Morrow plating need simplification for low-resolution readability. Early Aegis/Rei boards include model-invented historical-looking date/studio decorations; these are not real provenance. All assets were newly generated on October 21, 2026.

## Movement direction

The sources do not establish original frame timings. Existing Rift Duel timings are original game rules. Design pose changes around their exact contact frames. Fast does not mean every joint moves at the same speed: the supporting leg initiates, hips rotate, chest follows, arm strikes and hair/cloth settle later. Heavy fighters spend more visible time loading and recovering; agile fighters use narrower weight shifts; caster gestures should show deliberate body mechanics.

In this first pass the 16-cell sheets show move thumbnails, not complete cycles. A complete production set still needs walk/retreat/circle cycles, jump/landing, each combo's intermediate keys, individual capture contexts, reactions and cinematic camera coverage. Do not claim those are finished because all major action categories have one drawing.
